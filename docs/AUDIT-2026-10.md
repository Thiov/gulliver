# Gulliver audit, 24 October 2026

A full read-through of all 154 source files, split into five areas: size state and sync, movement and block physics, combat and items, carrying with commands and config, and client rendering. Each claim about vanilla behaviour was checked against the decompiled Minecraft 26.2 source, and claims about the older versions against the 1.20.1 / 1.21.x / 26.1.2 jars. Nothing has been changed yet.

Severity: **High** = breaks the game for someone or loses something permanently. **Medium** = clearly wrong behaviour players will notice. **Low** = edge case or polish.

## High

| # | Where | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | `ShoulderHelper`, `MixinEntity` (carry) | A survival player held in mid-air on a dedicated server is kicked with "Flying is not enabled on this server" after about 4 seconds, because carried players aren't vanilla passengers. The server also rubber-bands them with "moved wrongly". Singleplayer and LAN are fine. |
| H2 | `ShoulderHelper.place` | A carried player gets `noPhysics`, so a hacked client being carried can send positions through walls and break free on the other side. This matters on PvP servers. |
| H3 | `MixinCameraShake`, `TremorHandler` | On **1.20.1 and 1.21.10** the screen shake stops working after about 28–55 minutes of play. The shake timer grows forever, and the older `Mth.sin(float)` saturates once its argument gets large enough. |
| H4 | `GulliverEnvoy` floor cracking | Giants crack "brittle" floors detected by the glass sound, which also covers **End portal frames and Nether portals**, plus ice, glowstone, sea lanterns and redstone lamps. There is no check for unbreakable blocks, so a giant can destroy an End portal frame. |
| H5 | `GiantAoe` crater, growth burst, floor cracking | Collateral blocks are removed without firing the loader's break events. That means claim and protection mods can't stop them and adventure mode is ignored. Only the block you actually punch is protected. |
| H6 | `MixinLivingEntityClimbRope` | Any **normal-size** player holding a lead next to a wall counts as "on a ladder". They take no fall damage dropping down a 1×1 shaft and walk about 30% slower along walls. The 1.6.4 original only did this while you pushed into the wall. |

## Medium

**Size and sync**
- M1: Potions and dyes ignore the configured size limits. With `maxPlayerSize: 2`, Strong Embiggening still makes you 8×.
- M2: Respawn ignores body size, so a giant respawning in a bedroom gets stuck in the ceiling and suffocates. With karma mode the respawn even bursts the ceiling.
- M3: The upper half of big giants is invisible to entity lookups (vanilla only searches 4 blocks below and 0 above the feet's chunk section). Arrows and tridents fly through a giant's chest and head.
- M4: Mob conversions lose the size, so a giant zombie that drowns becomes a normal drowned. The same happens to villager→witch, cured zombie villagers, and tadpole→frog.

**Movement**
- M5: The 1.5× sneak-jump works at every size except exactly 1.0, so size 0.99 sneak-jumps 2.6 blocks.
- M6: The vanilla sprint-jump boost isn't scaled, so a tiny can bunny-hop about 2.2× faster than it sprints.
- M7: Rain drowning ignores Water Breathing, Conduit Power, Respiration, creative mode and mobs that breathe underwater (undead, turtles, axolotls).
- M8: Slabs, beds and other half-height blocks count as rain shelter for the smallest sizes, because their eye is inside the block.
- M9: The giant landing crush triggers on any fall over 3 blocks. From size 4.5 that includes every normal jump. It also ignores slime blocks, hay bales and honey, so landing on a cushion still crushes it.
- M10: Paper gliding also clamps creative flight (you can't fly down) and elytra dives.

**Combat and items**
- M11: Crater blocks ignore Silk Touch and Fortune, and the growth burst drops ores without any tool check.
- M12: Item pickup becomes strict at any size below 1.0, not only for tinies. At 0.99 pickup range drops from about 1.4 to 0.42 blocks.
- M13 (1.20.1): Giant saturation is divided by size² instead of size.
- M14 (1.20.1): Tiny eating is silent for most of the time and the food is drawn off-screen. Tinies also eat slower even though the comment says faster.
- M15 (Fabric): When another mod vetoes damage, the attacker-context stack never pops. It leaks, and later knockback reads a stale attacker.
- M16 (1.21.11, 26.1.2): Sweep-attack knockback ignores the attacker's size.

**Carrying, config, commands**
- M17: Carried creatures aren't released properly on dimension change. A carried NoAI mob stays frozen and can't be picked up again.
- M18: The V-key pickup bypasses claim mods and ownership, so anyone can grab another player's tamed wolf.
- M19: A typo in `gulliver.json` makes the game overwrite the whole file with defaults. `/gulliver reload` also reports success when parsing failed.

**Client**
- M20: The inventory player preview is scaled by your size: a belly close-up at 8× and a speck at 1/8 on 1.21.10, and a model covering the whole screen on 1.20.1. Held items in the preview are wrong on the newer versions.
- M21: The third-person camera ignores the mount's size, so riding a size-8 mob puts the camera inside it.
- M22: Screen shake has no setting, ignores vanilla's accessibility options (Damage Tilt, Distortion Effects), keeps running while paused and on the death screen, and isn't damped while string-riding a giant.

## Low

- Config: no validation. `NaN`/`Infinity` crashes at start-up, and spawn strings like `","` or `"-"` crash the server on the first entity tick.
- Monsters that are not `Monster` subclasses (slimes, ghasts, phantoms, shulkers, hoglins) use the animal size settings.
- Attributes are set from the unclamped size in `setBaseSize` and fixed later by the safety net.
- 1.20.1: stale potion size if the game saves between an effect ending and the next tick.
- Spectators can resize themselves through the packet. `/gulliver get` has no permission check even though the README says it is operator-only.
- A size range starting at 0 (`"0-2"`) rolls exactly 0 one time in nine, and the command fails partway.
- Dyes and mushrooms swallow the use packet even when dye resizing is off.
- Setting a creature down on a wall face puts it half inside the wall.
- A sneaking player isn't protected when pickup and throw arrive in the same tick, and a dead carrier can still grab.
- A phantom carried creature after a V-key race stops you attacking until you relog.
- The fall threshold ignores the `safe_fall_distance` attribute, so resized foxes and horses lose their bonus.
- Griefing gaps: giants break cobwebs without a spawn-protection check, the landing crush ignores adventure mode, and farmland trampling skips the Forge/NeoForge trample event (items trample too).
- Rope climbing: speed isn't scaled (tinies climb about 8× faster per body length), spectators can climb, you can cling under ceilings, and holding string turns off gliding and the raft.
- `MixinEntityPushOut` never affects players (vanilla only calls that method for items and XP orbs).
- Snow: 8 layers gives extra-tinies the 7-layer shape (off by one).
- Resized horses and rabbits jump at vanilla height.
- At 8× walking and sprinting look the same, because the walk-animation speed is capped.
- Tinies are slowed by soul sand under water (vanilla excludes water), a tiny standing in a tripped tripwire keeps it powered, and gliders get the heat updraft twice.
- Bees never actually attack tinies (vanilla bees only attack when angry). Spiders hunt every tiny creature, including tiny spiders, and ignore line of sight.
- Mobs that flee also ignore small threats, so a tiny cat doesn't scare creepers.
- Tinies can open ender chests without a pointy item.
- A sword in one hand doesn't stop bare-hand TNT priming with the other hand.
- Pointy-weapon damage checks only the main hand. The Fabric giant splash fires before claim mods can cancel the attack. 1.20.1 has no crossbow scaling at all.
- Trampling and giant splash hit your own pets and teammates.
- Your own sounds are never volume-scaled (only everyone else's).
- View bobbing jitters during a size change while walking.
- The shake is applied after the culling frustum is built, so chunks at the screen edge can pop during big quakes.
- The first-person glider paper ignores size, and the paper and raft props smear for one frame after a teleport.
- 1.20.1: giants' first-person hands can be clipped by the scaled near plane.
- Compatibility: every mixin injection is hard-required, so a camera mod that touches the same method crashes the game instead of losing a cosmetic effect. `MixinItemInHandRenderer` can leave the pose stack unbalanced if another mod cancels rendering.
- Size modifiers on attributes are permanent, so they stay on players if Gulliver is removed (160 HP and 8× reach forever).
- Per-entity overrides use only the entity's path, so `othermod:zombie` and `zombie` collide.

## What the wider size range (1/32 to 32) needs

These aren't bugs today, but they must be fixed when the range opens up:

- **Sync:** the client currently clamps sizes with its own config, so the server has to send its limits on join and on reload. Old config files already contain 0.125/8 and would pin the old limits, so they need a migration.
- **Guards against hangs:** size 0 or NaN makes the step-height loop run forever. The range check must reject both.
- **Knockback:** the attacker/target ratio has no cap. At 32× against 1/32 it is about 1000×, which flings mobs hundreds of blocks per tick.
- **Block destruction volume:**
  - Growing from 8× to 32× would sweep about 21,000 blocks with drops in one tick.
  - The landing crush at 32× is about 2,200 blocks per jump.
  - Both need per-tick budgets.
- **Rendering:**
  - The near plane gets too small below 1/8, so distant terrain flickers. It needs a lower limit.
  - At 32× the near plane is 1.6 blocks, which clips nearby blocks and the hands.
  - Name-tag (64 blocks) and shadow (16 blocks) distances are fixed, so they need scaling for giants.
- **Vanilla caps:** reach stops scaling at about 14× (the vanilla attribute maximum is 64) and max health caps at 1024.
- **Extremes that get absurd:**
  - Eating at 32× takes 1 tick.
  - Below 1/16 a pointy item no longer opens doors.
  - Every food gives at least 1 nutrition, so for big giants a cookie equals a steak.
  - Glide descent hits vanilla's dead zone at 1/32.
  - Shake would be constant for a 1/32 viewer.
- **Small details:** the config screen slider stops, number formatting (1/32 shows as 0.031), and the throw cap.
