# Cave Dweller (Fabric/NeoForge Port)

An unofficial port of **Cave Dweller Evolved** to Fabric and NeoForge.

Something tall and pale lives in the dark down there. You'll hear it before you see it, and it doesn't stop at walls, gaps or doors.

## Features

- The Cave Dweller, with its original model, animations and sounds
- Spawn timing, light levels, dimensions and biomes are all configurable in `config/cave_dweller-server.json`
- A spawn egg for testing

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.20.1, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.2 or 26.2**, pick the file for your version
- **Fabric** (with Fabric API) or **NeoForge** (Forge on 1.20.1)
- **GeckoLib** for your loader and version
